# 📐 Present Simple — настоящее простое
T|<b>Present Simple</b> — привычки, регулярные действия и факты: <i>I drink coffee every morning. The sun rises in the east.</i>
T|Утверждение: <b>V1</b>, а для he/she/it — <b>V1 + s/es</b>: <i>She works. He watches TV.</i>
T|Вопрос: <b>Do/Does + подлежащее + V1</b>: <i>Do you like tea? Does he work here?</i> Отрицание: <b>don't / doesn't + V1</b>: <i>I don't know. She doesn't smoke.</i>
T|Подсказки: <i>every day, usually, always, often, sometimes, never, on Mondays</i>.
Q|My brother ___ in a bank.|works|work|is working|He → works (Present Simple, добавляем -s).
Q|We ___ coffee every morning.|drink|drinks|are drink|Привычка, we → drink без -s.
Q|___ she speak Spanish?|Does|Do|Is|Вопрос для she начинается с Does.
Q|He ___ like horror movies.|doesn't|don't|isn't|Отрицание для he → doesn't.
Q|The train ___ at 8:15 every day.|leaves|leave|is leaving|Расписание и регулярность → leaves.
Q|They never ___ late.|come|comes|are come|They → come, без -s.
Q|What time ___ you usually get up?|do|does|are|Вопрос с you → do.
Q|Water ___ at 100 degrees.|boils|boil|is boiling|Факт, it → boils.
# 📐 Present Continuous — настоящее длительное
T|<b>Present Continuous</b> — действие прямо сейчас или временная ситуация: <i>I'm cooking dinner now. We're staying at a hotel this week.</i>
T|Форма: <b>am / is / are + V-ing</b>: <i>She is reading. They are playing.</i> Вопрос: <i>Are you working?</i> Отрицание: <i>He isn't sleeping.</i>
T|Подсказки: <i>now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, Look! Listen!</i>
T|Правописание: make → making (e пропадает), run → running (согласная удваивается), lie → lying.
Q|Look! It ___ outside.|is snowing|snows|snow|«Look!» — происходит сейчас → is snowing.
Q|I can't talk now. I ___ dinner.|am cooking|cook|cooks|Сейчас, I → am cooking.
Q|What ___ you doing right now?|are|do|is|Вопрос с you в Continuous → are.
Q|She ___ TV at the moment.|isn't watching|doesn't watching|not watches|Отрицание Continuous → isn't + V-ing.
Q|They ___ in London this week.|are staying|stay|stays|Временная ситуация → are staying.
Q|___ he sleeping?|Is|Does|Are|He → Is.
Q|We ___ to music now.|are listening|listen|listens|now → are listening.
Q|The kids ___ in the garden right now.|are playing|plays|play|Мн. число, сейчас → are playing.
# 📐 Past Simple — прошедшее простое
T|<b>Past Simple</b> — действие, которое закончилось в прошлом: <i>yesterday, last week, two days ago</i>.
T|Правильные глаголы: <b>+ed</b> (walk → walked). Неправильные — свои формы (go → went), см. раздел «📘 Глаголы».
T|Вопрос: <b>Did</b> + подлежащее + V1: <i>Did you see Mira?</i> Отрицание: <b>didn't</b> + V1: <i>We didn't go out.</i>
T|Примеры: <i>Yesterday we walked in the park.</i> — Вчера мы гуляли в парке. <i>I bought a ticket.</i> — Я купил(а) билет.
Q|Yesterday we ___ to the park.|went|go|goed|go — неправильный глагол, V2 = went.
Q|I ___ eat breakfast this morning.|didn't|doesn't|not|Отрицание в прошедшем: didn't + V1.
Q|___ you see the movie last night?|Did|Do|Were|Вопрос в Past Simple начинается с Did.
Q|I ___ a letter yesterday.|wrote|writed|write|write → wrote (неправильный глагол).
Q|She ___ the dishes after dinner.|washed|washing|wash|Правильный глагол: wash + ed.
Q|We ___ a great movie last week.|saw|seen|see|see → saw (V2), seen — это V3.
Q|Where ___ you go on vacation last year?|did|do|were|Вопрос в прошедшем: did + подлежащее + V1.
Q|He ___ come to the party yesterday.|didn't|doesn't|wasn't|Отрицание в прошедшем: didn't + V1.
# 📐 Past Continuous — прошедшее длительное
T|<b>Past Continuous</b> — действие, которое длилось в определённый момент в прошлом: <i>At 8 p.m. I was cooking.</i>
T|Форма: <b>was / were + V-ing</b>: <i>I was reading. They were playing.</i> Вопрос: <i>Were you sleeping?</i> Отрицание: <i>She wasn't working.</i>
T|Часто вместе с Past Simple: длинное действие (Continuous) прерывается коротким (Simple): <i>I was walking when it started to rain.</i>
T|Подсказки: <i>while, when, at 5 o'clock yesterday, all evening</i>.
Q|At 8 p.m. yesterday, I ___ dinner.|was cooking|cooked|am cooking|Конкретный момент в прошлом → was cooking.
Q|They ___ football when it started to rain.|were playing|played|are playing|Длинное действие, которое прервали → were playing.
Q|___ you sleeping when I called?|Were|Did|Was|You → Were.
Q|She ___ TV while he was cooking.|was watching|watched|is watching|Два действия одновременно → was watching.
Q|I ___ when the phone rang.|was sleeping|slept|am sleeping|Длилось, когда его прервал звонок → was sleeping.
Q|We ___ working at that time.|weren't|wasn't|didn't|We → weren't.
Q|What ___ he doing at 5 o'clock?|was|did|is|He → was.
Q|I ___ along the street when I saw an old friend.|was walking|walked|were walking|I → was walking (длинное действие прервано).
